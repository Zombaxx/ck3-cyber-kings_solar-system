# Cyber Kings (2026-11-17): The American Question - the United States as one federal people divided into culture blocs.
# Contrast with the European Question (a union of sovereign states that leave by agreement): here there is no peaceful exit -
# secession means civil war; crises centralise power in the president; expansion is continental (Manifest Destiny toward the
# Hegemony of the Americas, to rival Eurasia); elections every 4 years move a polarization score; breakaways are corporate
# free cities and culture-bloc successor states. Blocs (red / blue / swing) come from culture traditions.
# Phases follow the American cycles (crisis -> high -> awakening -> unraveling): Status Quo (start), Era of Good Feelings,
#   Great Awakening, Gilded Age, Great Depression, Reform Era, American Century, Polarization, Secession Crisis, Civil War,
#   Reconstruction, Imperial Presidency, Manifest Destiny; speculative: Balkanization, Corporate Secession, Progressive
#   Commonwealth; Foreign Dominion when ruled from outside.
# Design: _dev/DLC_ADAPTATION.md section 18. Catalysts: common/on_action/cyber_american_on_actions.txt and on_yearly.
