# Cyber Kings (2026-11-17): The European Question - the European Union goes through the cycles of European history:
# concert -> integration -> expansion (colonies and conquests) -> rivalry -> great war -> concert ...
# Breakup and confederal paths (2026-11-17 19:30): rivalry or great war -> dissolution (members leave, the Union can be
# destroyed) -> concert of separate states; a European Confederation can end the concert of separate states, and a
# confederation that grows or defends Europe against outside foes moves on to integration (a new Union, without war).
# Imperium (2026-11-17 19:40): a Union ruler with full crown authority can dissolve the European Parliament and make the
# Union a true empire (ck_proclaim_european_imperium_decision): no member can walk out while it lasts, only war breaks it.
# Outside rule (2026-11-17 20:10): an outsider holding the Union (or its ruler as vassal/tributary, or half of Europe) puts
# Europe under Foreign Dominion; a Hegemon of Terra holding the Union locks it in Imperium; a European hegemon makes it an
# Imperium. These are set by ck_european_question_yearly_effect, not by catalysts.
# (Concert of Europe / Westphalia / the EU's founding; Carolingian and EU integration; the ages of empire and colonies;
#  the great-power rivalries before 1618, 1792 and 1914; the general wars that end them.) No final winner.
# Design: _dev/DLC_ADAPTATION.md section 7b. Catalysts: common/on_action/cyber_situations_on_actions.txt and on_yearly.
